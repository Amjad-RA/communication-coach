💬 Communication Coach

Your personal communication coach

Pick a skill, practice for 10 minutes a day, and build confidence step by step. Each session opens in Gemini with a ready-made coaching prompt.

How to use

Every session follows the same simple 10-minute plan.

  1. Open English Practice or Public Speaking and find today's day card.
  2. Read the topic and say the useful words / phrases out loud once.
  3. Tap the Gemini button. The prompt is copied and Gemini opens in a new tab.
  4. If the chat box is empty, paste (Ctrl+V / long-press → Paste) and send.
  5. Use Gemini's microphone or Live mode to speak your answers.
  6. Come back and tap Mark as done to track your progress.

The 10-minute plan

  1. Warm-up · 2 min
    Two easy questions on the topic.
  2. Main practice · 6 min
    Conversation or role-play, one question at a time, with gentle corrections.
  3. Feedback · 2 min
    Scores, your top 3 mistakes and one sentence to remember.
