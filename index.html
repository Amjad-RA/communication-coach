How to use

Every session is 10 minutes with Gemini as your coach.

  1. Tap ▶ Start on Home, or open English or Speaking and find today's card.
  2. Read the topic and say the useful words / phrases out loud once.
  3. Tap the Gemini button at the top-right of the card. The prompt is copied and Gemini opens.
  4. If Gemini's chat box is empty, paste (long-press → Paste, or Ctrl+V) and send.
  5. Use Gemini's microphone or Live mode to speak your answers.
  6. Need a quick boost? Open Tips for communication tricks and pronunciation drills. For sound tips, use Gemini Live so Gemini can hear you.
  7. Come back and tap Mark as done to grow your streak 🔥.

The 100-day English plan

  1. 🚀 Phase 1 · Fast Track · Days 1–30
    Speak with confidence, fast. Fluency first — only your biggest mistakes get corrected.
  2. 🔒 Phase 2 · Lock It In · Days 31–100
    10 themed weeks to make your English accurate, natural and permanent.
  3. Every Phase 2 week
    5 new topics → 🔧 Fix-It day (your repeated mistakes) → 🔁 Review day (revisits earlier topics).
  4. 🎓 Day 99 & 100
    A final speaking test with a level estimate, then your English-for-life routine.

The 100-day speaking plan

  1. 🚀 Phase 1 · Fast Track · Days 1–30
    Get up and speak with confidence: stage fear, voice, structure, stories and your first big speech.
  2. 🔒 Phase 2 · Lock It In · Days 31–100
    10 weeks: daily habit, voice, structure, storytelling, persuasion, occasions, work talks, thinking on your feet and stage presence.
  3. Every Phase 2 week
    5 new skills → 🔧 Fix-It day (your speaking habits) → 🔁 Review day (redo early sessions).
  4. 🎓 Days 94–100
    Build and deliver your 5-minute signature talk, a final speaking test and your speaker-for-life plan.

The 10-minute plan

  1. Warm-up / Recall · 2 min
    Two easy questions — or, in Phase 2, recall words and sentences from your last session.
  2. Main practice · 6 min
    Conversation or role-play, one question at a time, with corrections.
  3. Feedback · 2 min
    Scores, your top 3 mistakes and one sentence to remember.
